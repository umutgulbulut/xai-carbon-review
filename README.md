# XAI for Carbon-Related Prediction and Forecasting – Supplementary Material

Supplementary material for the paper:

> **Explainable Artificial Intelligence for Carbon-Related Prediction and Forecasting: A Systematic Literature Review and Multidimensional Taxonomy**
> U. Bulut, E. Özceylan, P. Kocabey Çiftçi, Ö. N. Kenger
> Submitted to ISAS 2026.

The review was conducted in accordance with the PRISMA 2020 guidelines. Scopus and the Web of Science Core Collection were searched (final search: 25 October 2026). After filtering and duplicate removal, 212 records were screened, 202 full texts were assessed for eligibility, and 71 studies were included.

## Files

| File | Description |
|---|---|
| `S1_screening_results.pdf` | Full-text screening results of all 212 records (Scopus: 203; WoS only: 9), with extracted study characteristics and the screening decision |
| `S2_excluded_studies.pdf` | Records excluded at full-text assessment (n = 131) with the primary exclusion reason, the reports not retrieved (n = 10), and a summary by reason |
| `S3_taxonomy_concise.pdf` | Concise taxonomy of the 71 included studies: application area, prediction task, models, XAI methods, and performance metrics |
| `S4_taxonomy_categorized.pdf` | Categorization of the included studies into the taxonomy dimensions of Table III (domain, target, modeling paradigm, best model, XAI techniques, explanation level, explanation reliability), with a summary |
| `S5_taxonomy_extended.pdf` | Extended taxonomy: explanation level, data type, model complexity, XAI findings, reported limitations, and future research directions |
